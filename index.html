Context words

A glossary of unique terms (product names, features, jargon). During Browser dictation, recognized words that sound ≈ a glossary entry are auto-corrected to the exact spelling — so the recognizer invents less.

Dictionary 0 words
Term = exact spelling inserted into the report (e.g. Follistim, New Leaf).
Sounds like = comma-separated variants the recognizer might output, incl. how it sounds in Ukrainian (e.g. фоллістім, фолістім). Used for matching only.
Click 🔍 Analyze Template to auto-extract terms (the AI also fills the Ukrainian variants).
Matching
Higher = stricter (fewer corrections, fewer false hits). Lower = more aggressive matching. 85% is a good default.
